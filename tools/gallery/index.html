Asset Catalogue

Every model and surface made for the game so far, photographed on its own under a neutral studio light with the game's tone mapping. This page grows as new batches land. Recoloured parts (upholstery and paint) get a different colour for each piece in game.

Batch log

Batch 7 · 2 Nov 2026

People you can talk to: strangers until you introduce yourself, then they'll tell you what they do, where they live, who they live with, the town's news, and the way to places. The shoreline gets wet sand and washing foam.

Batch 6 · 2 Nov 2026

Water with moving ripples, sky reflections and sun glints on the sea and lakes. Speeders with riders run the roads in and out of town. Firms' ships make a daily cargo run: they lift off their pads, fly out of sight and come back hours later. And rooms look lived in: skirting boards, rugs, framed paintings, floor lamps, tiled splashbacks, pin boards.

Batch 5 · 2 Nov 2026

A second ship: a 34 m freighter with a tall cargo hold behind a wide ramp, a crew deck and a two-seat flight deck; one stands on a cargo field outside every town. Buildings got downpipes, balconies, AC units, vents, roof stair housings and solar panels. Grass now grows in clumps you can walk through, and there is weather: a drifting cloud deck you can fly through and above, grey overcast, and rain.

Batch 4 · 2 Nov 2026

The shuttle, rebuilt outside: panelled plating with seams, rivets and worn edges, a ceramic heat-shield belly, a ribbed dorsal spine, greebles, slatted vents, RCS thruster blocks, flared engine bells with a burn deep in the throat, hydraulic landing gear, red and green nav lights, hazard markings and a framed windscreen. The speeder takes the same plating. And the open land now has plants: conifers, broadleaf trees and shrubs made of leaf and needle cards, boulders, and flat stand-ins for distant woods.

Batch 3 · 2 Nov 2026

The town and the land. Ten new surfaces replace the old generated textures on every building, street and hillside: precast panels, corrugated steel, render, floor tiles, asphalt, pavers, and grass, dry ground, rock and gravel for the terrain (rock is projected from three sides, so cliffs don't smear). A street light model replaces the placeholder. Windows are dressed one by one: blinds, curtains, a dim room behind. The shuttle has a windscreen you can see out of, and a cockpit view in flight.

Batch 2 · 2 Nov 2026

A speeder bike and a shuttle, both rideable in game. The shuttle's model is built from the same layout file the game uses for its interior: hold, cabin and cockpit sit exactly where you walk. The paint texture was toned down; its scratches read as cracks on large surfaces.

Batch 1 · 2 Nov 2026

Furniture and fixtures for homes, shops, offices and workshops, plus the twelve tileable surfaces they're made from. All 33 pieces are in game, fitted to the same footprints as the old grey boxes.

In game

The same models where they are used. Studio renders hide everything that goes wrong under real light, so each batch is checked here too.

Surfaces

Seamless PBR textures (colour, surface relief, roughness and metalness), shown on a 1 m sphere beside a 1.2 m slab so you can judge their real scale. The tile size is how far each texture runs before it repeats.

Furniture & fixtures

Sizes are length × depth × height in metres. Triangle counts are for one piece.
